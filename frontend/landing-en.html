Four policy instruments

Simulate what
actually matters

Each instrument maps to a real policy decision. Change one or combine them all — the model recalculates instantly.

01

Subject-Option Ratio

Target share of teachers deployed within their trained subject option — directly controls specialisation quality.

02

Annual Teaching Hours

Total subject teaching hours scheduled per class each year — affects how many teachers are needed per school.

03

Teacher Hour Capacity

Maximum annual teaching hours a single teacher can carry — raising this reduces the headcount required.

04

Co-teaching Share

Proportion of classes team-taught by two teachers — expands coverage without adding permanent positions.
